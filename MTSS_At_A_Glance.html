MTSS Overview

The process for requesting academic and behavior support at Wendell Phillips 63.

Process Overview

The Request Sequence

Bring the student to a collaborative culture meeting first. Try Tier 1 and collaborative supports together. Track for about three weeks. If the need continues, complete the MTSS Behavior Request and we build a plan.

You are not expected to carry any part of this sequence alone. The team works alongside you at every step.

Safety events skip all of this. Go straight to Level 3, secure safety, and contact the office now.

Classification

Behavior Level Definitions

Level 1 · Classroom-managed

Minor, in-the-moment. Redirects, off-task, small conflicts. Stays in class. Use your Teacher Response toolkit. No request needed.

Level 2 · Restorative

A pattern that continues after Tier 1, and you can say what you tried. Routes to the Restorative Room. Complete the MTSS Behavior Request first.

Level 3 · Office / Safety

Aggression, hands on others, elopement, threats. The office responds now. Secure safety, contact the office immediately. Family contact and investigation follow. Level 3 does not route to Restorative.

Restorative Support

Tap-In and Tap-Out

Tap-In

Support comes to you. The student stays in your room. Use this whenever it is workable — keeping a student with their class is almost always the better outcome, and a well-timed tap-in often prevents what would have become a tap-out.

Tap-Out

The student leaves to the Restorative Room, regulates, and returns.

Both are on the MTSS Behavior Request. Both are tracked. Neither is a failure on your part.

Prior to Submission

The Bias Pause

Before requesting support for a subjective behavior — defiance, disrespect, or disruption — pause and ask:

Would I read this the same way for any student in my room?

This is not an accusation. Those three categories are where referral disproportionality shows up in every school that measures it, because all three require a judgment call about intent and tone. The pause is a design feature, not a suspicion about you. If you are unsure, talk with a counselor before you request.

Tier 1 Documentation

Required Before Submission

  1. Bring the student to a collaborative culture meeting. Before paperwork. This is where most concerns get solved.
  2. Try Tier 1 supports, with the team's help. Proximity, nonverbal cue, private redirect, offered choice, calm corner or break, changed task or scaffold, regulation strategy, restorative conversation, seat change, call home.
  3. Track for about three weeks. Rough notes are fine — dates and what you tried.
  4. Complete the MTSS Behavior Request, if the need continues. It asks what you tried because you will already have the answer.
Available Supports

Section F Requests

  • Restorative support — tap-in or tap-out
  • Collaborative culture meeting
  • A DBRC
  • Check-In / Check-Out
  • Counselor check-in
  • An MTSS behavior plan

You request these on the form. The multidisciplinary team decides and assigns an owner — that is what protects you from carrying a plan alone.

Review Process

After Submission

The multidisciplinary team — Director of Culture, counselors, support specialists — reviews within 5 school days and comes back to you with a decision and a named owner.

If you flagged it Urgent for a safety or crisis concern, you hear from someone the same day.
