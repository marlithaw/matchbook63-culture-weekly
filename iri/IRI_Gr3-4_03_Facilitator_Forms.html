Form C · Lesson Cards · Set 1 of 2Scenario Cards
Read aloud during the lesson. Use one per session;
a returning student gets a new card. Cut on the dashed lines.
Scenario · Fairness
1
The teacher changes the line order. Your friend gets the line-leader spot you usually have.
PauseThe moment it feels unfair, before refusing to move.
ChooseTake the new spot · say "I'm mad" out loud · ask the teacher about it later
RepairRejoin the line. Tell the teacher what was upsetting.
Scenario · Bumped
2
Someone steps on the back of your shoe in the hallway. Your shoe comes off.
PauseRight before turning around to push.
ChooseCheck if it was an accident · say "Hey, watch out" · fix the shoe and keep walking
RepairIf hands went on someone, check in with them with an adult there.
Scenario · Family
3
A classmate says something about your mom in front of other kids.
PauseWhen the anger rises in your chest.
ChooseSay "Don't talk about my family" · step away · get an adult
RepairTalk it through with the classmate and an adult present.
Scenario · Laughed at
4
You miss the ball in gym. Someone laughs and says you didn't even get close.
PauseHearing the laugh, before saying something back.
ChooseLet it go for now · tell the coach · say "That wasn't kind"
RepairIf you said something back, take it back with that classmate.
Form C · Lesson Cards · Set 2 of 2Scenario Cards
Read aloud during the lesson. Use one per session;
a returning student gets a new card. Cut on the dashed lines.
Scenario · Called out
5
The teacher asks for quiet and says your name in front of the whole class after a few reminders.
PauseFeeling singled out, before talking back.
ChooseLower your voice · ask for a break · talk to the teacher after class
RepairTell the teacher privately what felt hard about that moment.
Scenario · The score
6
At recess, two classmates argue with you about the soccer score and get in your face.
PauseWhen everyone's voices start getting louder.
ChooseWalk to the other side · ask an adult to settle it · let the score go
RepairSay "We're good" or shake hands before class starts.
Scenario · Stuck
7
Work time starts and your pencil is gone. Everyone else is already working.
PauseBefore deciding to quit the work.
ChooseUse the class pencil procedure · ask a neighbor · raise your hand
RepairFinish the work that was missed.
Scenario · Not safe
8
You hear that some kids plan to jump you after school.
PauseWhen fear makes you want to strike first.
ChooseTell a trusted adult right away · stay near adults
RepairNo repair needed. Getting help is the right choice.
FacilitatorIf a student shares a real threat, stop, thank them, and tell the Deans today.
