Where these numbers come from, and what is left out
Source
September 14 through October 2 and November 2–6 come straight from the PowerSchool Attendance Summary by Grade reports, weekly and year-to-date. Elementary and middle are combined as days attended divided by (actual days minus days not enrolled), the same formula PowerSchool uses for ADA%. Every other week is the figure published in that week's Culture Weekly attendance snapshot.
Published figures vs. PowerSchool
Where both exist, the PowerSchool reports run later come in higher than what was published that week, most of all for Kindergarten and Grade 2 in late September. PowerSchool keeps changing as absences are corrected or coded after the fact, so a report run weeks later can differ from one run on Friday. Weeks still taken from the newsletter could shift if their reports are rerun.
Week of Aug 17–21
The campus figure is the one published in Week 4. Grade figures for that week were not published as a table, so they are worked back from the Week 5 "change vs. previous week" column.
Short weeks
Oct 5–8 and Nov 2–6 were four-day weeks, so a single absence moves those rates more.
Left out
July 30 through August 14: no weekly figure on hand, so the line starts August 17. October 12–16: fall break. The elementary and middle split is missing for Aug 17–21. An elementary report covering Sep 25 to Oct 2 overlaps two weeks and is not used.
