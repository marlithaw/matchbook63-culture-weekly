Sources, definitions and limits
Referrals
Every Restorative Room Entry Form submission for a student in grades 3–8. Per 100 present: referrals divided by enrollment times that week's grade attendance (PowerSchool Attendance Summary by Grade and Culture Weekly snapshots), per school day. Part of the jump at launch is more complete reporting.
Sent out twice, approved reason, step named first, where it comes from
From the Restorative Referral Analysis sorting, which matches students by an identity-resolved key and runs through Nov 4.
IRI
New placements from the Intensive Restorative Intervention Daily Roster, one per student per day, cancelled entries left out. Grades 3–4 joined in October.
LiveSchool
Share of students with at least one positive point that week, grades 5–8 only (store purchases and Hallway Hollers awards left out).
Left out
Weeks before Aug 17 (no weekly attendance figure), fall break Oct 12–16, Nov 3 (no school), and Tap Ins, which are not on the form.
