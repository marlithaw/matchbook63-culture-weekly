Round 1 · 1:10 to 1:22 · Regulation: the sleeper

Launch script · 1:10“Round one is Regulation. Teacher A, you have the teacher seat. Teacher B, you are the actor, so read your card and keep it to yourself. Teacher C, you are hunting moves and you get one call-out. Two minutes to set up. When you hear the signal, launch.”
1:10Set up, 2 min. Actor reads the the sleeper card privately. Teacher sets out the seating chart and lap marks. Move hunter takes the Move Hunt sheet.
1:12Run, 6 min. Teacher launches with a What to Do direction. Compliance lap (✓), then mastery lap (★ ○ ●). Same calm visit to the sleeper each time. One call-out from the move hunter.
1:18Debrief, 4 min. 1. Fourth member first. 2. Move hunter: moves found, when, and why that one. 3. Teacher: one move to keep, one move to change.
1:22Signal. Move to the Round 2 seats in the chart above.

Round 2 · 1:23 to 1:35 · Access and Clarity: “I just don’t understand”

Launch script · 1:23“Round two is Access and Clarity. Rotate one seat. Teacher B, you have the teacher seat. Teacher C, you are the actor. Teacher A, you are hunting moves. Teacher, have the dot strategy ready. Two minutes to set up.”
1:23Set up, 2 min. Actor reads the “I just don’t understand” card privately. Teacher sets out the seating chart and lap marks. Move hunter takes the Move Hunt sheet.
1:25Run, 6 min. Teacher launches and laps. On the mastery lap the actor says “I just don’t understand.” Teacher marks the chart and uses the dot strategy, then comes back around.
1:31Debrief, 4 min. 1. Fourth member first. 2. Move hunter: moves found, when, and why that one. 3. Teacher: one move to keep, one move to change.
1:35Signal. Move to the Round 3 seats in the chart above.

Round 3 · 1:36 to 1:48 · Full ladder: repeat disruption

Launch script · 1:36“Round three is the full ladder. Last rotation. Teacher C, you have the teacher seat. Teacher A, you are the actor. Teacher B, you are hunting moves, and after the warning you join the actor as a spectator. Tap In responders, you are on. When you hear the call, come in and hold the group.”
1:36Set up, 2 min. Actor reads the repeat disruption card privately. Teacher sets out the seating chart and lap marks. Move hunter takes the Move Hunt sheet.
1:38Run, 6 min. Warning, seat change, written reflection, Tap In call. Tally each step and keep teaching. The responder holds the group while the teacher takes the hallway conversation. Spectators return first.
1:44Debrief, 4 min. 1. Fourth member first. 2. Move hunter: moves found, when, and why that one. 3. Teacher: one move to keep, one move to change.
1:48Signal. Groups stay at their tables for the close.
