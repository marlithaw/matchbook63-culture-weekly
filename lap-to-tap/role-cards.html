Actor cards · 7
Row 2 · Right aisle · T1
Call-out

Work the task at first. When the attention getter plays and the room goes quiet, call out:

“This is stupid.”
  1. After the warning, mutter “whatever” under your breath.
  2. During the example, tap your pencil on the desk until the teacher stands next to you.
  3. After that, talk again. Move to Reset 1 when told.
  4. Talk again. Take the written reflection.
  5. Talk again until the Tap In is called. Answer Q1 and Q2 when they join.
  6. In the hallway, listen. Go back in last.

If the facilitator taps her clipboard twice in the hallway, refuse to go back in.

Row 3 · Left aisle · T2
Sleeper

Head down before the first lap.

  1. First visit: lift your head, then put it back down when the teacher walks away.
  2. Second visit: same.
  3. Third visit: sit up and start number 1.
Row 1 · Left aisle · R
Refuser

Do not start. Pencil down.

  1. When the teacher asks why, shrug.
  2. On the mastery lap, say: “I just don’t understand.”
  3. After the teacher shows you the dot, put a dot on number 1 and try number 2.
Row 4 · Left wall · Q1
Spectator

Finish fast. Plot A at (5, 2).

  1. After T1 gets the warning, react out loud (“Ooooh”).
  2. Keep talking to T1 after the Tap In call and the spectator notice.
  3. Go to the hallway when called. Go back in first.
Row 5 · Right wall · Q2
Spectator

Finish fast.

  1. After T1 gets the warning, laugh and turn around.
  2. Keep it going after the spectator notice.
  3. Go to the hallway when called. Go back in first.
Row 3 · Right wall · S
Lightning call-out 1

One call-out only, right after launch, before the teacher reaches you:

“Is this for a grade?”

Then do what the teacher says and work quietly for the rest of the model.

Row 6 · Right aisle · S
Lightning call-out 2

During the misconception example, call out without raising your hand:

“Wait, which one goes first?”

If the teacher gives a signal, follow it. Then work quietly.

Model roles · 2
Model · Restorative team
Tap In responder

Wait at the cafeteria door.

  1. When you hear “I need Tap In support with at least one student,” walk in and stand at the front.
  2. Hold the class on the task. Do not address the students involved.
  3. When the teacher steps into the hallway, you have the room.
  4. If the teacher steps aside to complete the voice form, stay until the teacher returns.
Model · Teacher station
LiveSchool point person

Stand at the teacher station.

  1. Every time the teacher names a student and gives a point, lead two snaps.
  2. Keep a tally of points given. Report the count in the debrief.
Practice set · print one per group (9)
Round 1 · Regulation
Actor: the sleeper

Head down before the first lap. Lift your head at each visit, then put it back down. Sit up and start on the third visit.

Round 2 · Access and Clarity
Actor: “I just don’t understand”

Do not start. Shrug when asked why. On the mastery lap, say “I just don’t understand.” After the dot strategy, try the next one.

Round 3 · Full ladder
Actor: repeat disruption

Call out after the attention getter. Keep talking through the warning, the seat change, and the written reflection until the Tap In is called. The lightning caller joins in as a spectator. Hallway, then return.

Every round
Lightning caller

One quick call-out per round, then quiet. Round 3: join the actor as a spectator after the warning.

Every round
Teacher

Seating chart and lap marks in hand. Launch, lap, pause, narrate, ladder. Use the scripts from the model. The teacher seat rotates each round.

Fourth member · Special education
Student with a plan code

Play a student whose seat carries a plan code. Respond to the plan steps the teacher uses. Open each debrief with one intervention strategy you use.

Fourth member · Specials
LiveSchool point person

Lead two snaps for every point. Play the lightning caller when the group needs one more student.

Every round
Tap In responder

Come in on the call. Hold the group on the task. Do not address the students involved. Stay if the teacher steps aside for the voice form.
