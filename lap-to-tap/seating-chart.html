Tally boxes on each seat: W = warning · SC = seat change · WR = written reflection · TI = Tap In. Names are invented for the model.

T Tier 2/3 · 4
Center aisle, rows 2–5. Reached in one pass down the aisle. Plan steps come first.
Q Quick finisher · 4
Wall seats, back half, spread apart. Checked last. Done early: check, then read or extended work.
R Reteach first · 6
Rows 1–2. First stop on the mastery lap. Room for a 90-second huddle.
S Striving · 14
Below level, working through the noise. The buffer. The chart protects their learning.
